🏆 PPV Prediction League – Quick Rules

PPVs Only

This league covers PPV events only across WWE, AEW, NXT, and TNA.

⏰ Deadline

  • Picks are due 2 HOURS before PPV start.
  • PPV cards are usually shared 24 hours before the event.
  • For WWE & AEW, the deadline matches the pre-show start time.
WWE PPV Scoring
  • Men’s World Title: 5pts
  • Men’s Tag Titles: 4 pts
  • Men’s Mid-Card Titles (IC / US): 4pts
  • ALL Women’s matches & titles: 4pt
  • Non-title matches: 4pt
AEW PPV Scoring
  • Men’s World Title: 5 pts
  • Men’s Tag Titles: 4 pts
  • TNT Title ONLY: 4 pts
  • ALL Women’s matches & other titles: 4pt
  • Non-title matches: 1 pt
TNA & NXT PPVs
  • ALL matches = 1 pt
  • No title bonuses

⚖️ Tie Breaker

  • If players are tied on points, the winner is decided by:
    Most points from WWE & AEW Title matches.
